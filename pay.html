Payments · Live

Meraxis Pay

Frictionless payments and billing infrastructure for modern teams. Ship subscriptions and one-time charges with a single API.

The problem

Product teams keep rebuilding billing. Plans change, usage appears, finance wants a different invoice, and the checkout that shipped last quarter no longer matches the product. The work is repetitive and still easy to get wrong.

What is live

Pay handles subscriptions, one-time charges, and usage meters that settle on the same invoice. Design partners use it as the billing primitive under their own product, not as a second dashboard their customers have to learn.

What we are watching

Whether teams treat usage billing as a feature they turn on, or as a reason to leave their current stack. The September release is the test. If partners still export events to a spreadsheet, the primitive is not small enough.
