Planned

GCC marketplace

A marketplace for Global Capability Centres where employees are verified before they take part.

The idea

Global Capability Centres concentrate talent and demand in one place — but commerce and side markets around them are often open to anyone. This experiment explores a marketplace where participation starts with employee verification.

What we’ll test

Whether verified membership creates enough trust and relevance for people to list, browse, and transact — and whether GCCs are the right wedge for that model.

Status

Planned. On the board; not validated yet.
