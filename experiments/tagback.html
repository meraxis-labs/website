TagBack

QR lost & found, privately.

Turn any item into a smart, private “return to owner” tag so honest finders can reach you—without ever revealing your personal contact details.

The idea

Lost items are recovered most often when a finder wants to help — and fails most often when the only option is a phone number or email printed on a label. TagBack sits in that gap: a QR tag that starts a private return flow without exposing how to reach you.

What we’re learning

Whether people will put tags on things they care about, whether finders will scan them, and whether a private relay is enough trust to make returns happen.

Status

TagBack is live. You can use the product today.
