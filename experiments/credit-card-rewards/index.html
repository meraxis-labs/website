Credit card rewards

A rewards programme built around credit card spend.

The idea

Credit card spend already carries rewards — but the programmes are fragmented, opaque, and hard to compare. This experiment asks whether a clearer rewards layer around that spend can earn attention and habit.

What we’ll test

Whether people will track or choose cards for rewards in a new way, and what “enough signal” looks like before building a full programme.

Status

Planned. On the board; not validated yet.
