Competition monitor

A tool that watches competitors and surfaces what changed.

The idea

Competitor tracking is usually manual or buried in noise. This experiment explores a monitor that watches selected competitors and surfaces what actually changed — pricing, messaging, features, presence.

What we’ll test

Whether teams will keep a short watchlist, which change signals matter, and whether a focused alert beat a general news feed.

Status

Planned. On the board; not validated yet.
