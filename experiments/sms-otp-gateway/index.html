SMS OTP gateway

A gateway for sending and verifying one-time passwords over SMS.

The idea

Many products still need SMS OTPs for login and verification. This experiment explores a focused gateway for sending and verifying those codes — with reliability and simplicity as the main bets.

What we’ll test

Whether builders want a dedicated OTP path, what delivery and verification matter most, and whether the wedge is strong enough to justify building beyond a thin prototype.

Status

Planned. On the board; not validated yet.
