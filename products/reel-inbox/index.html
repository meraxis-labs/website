Reel inbox

Send a reel in, then search what you saved — the same job JustDM does.

The idea

Useful reels disappear into DMs and saves. Reel inbox explores a simple loop: send a reel in, keep it, and find it again later by searching what you saved.

What we’ll test

Whether people will route reels into a dedicated inbox, how they want to search and organise them, and whether that habit sticks beyond a first save.

Status

Planned. On the board; not validated yet.
