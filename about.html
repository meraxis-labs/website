Why Meraxis Labs exists

Most useful problems are recognized and then left alone. The gap between seeing the problem and putting a product in someone’s hands is filled with delay. This lab is a way through that gap.

The philosophy

The way to understand a problem is to build something a real user can operate. Not a deck about the solution. Pay, Scan, and Vault exist because billing, verification, and secrets were problems we could put in front of teams and measure.

AI-assisted development made that realistic for a small lab. Prototypes that once needed a long staffing plan can be in a pilot quickly. The question that remains is whether the problem is worth the product.

Why the work is public

Research, methodology, and the journal are published as the work happens. Internally, the next experiment inherits the last one. Externally, collaborators can see how a decision was made, including when the decision was to stop.

Why some ideas are archived

Kill criteria are defined before the build. Missing the threshold frees the team and becomes a written result. A lab that ships every idea is not running experiments. Ledger will be archived if design partners do not describe a reconciliation pain strong enough to leave the spreadsheet.
