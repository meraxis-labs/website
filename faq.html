About the lab

What is Meraxis Labs?

An independent startup experimentation lab. We identify problems, validate them, build products, and publish the journey. Pay, Scan, and Vault are the live products.

How do you choose what to build?

From a problem someone is already working around, specific enough to falsify. The problem board is the intake. A solution looking for a market does not start an experiment.

How fast do you build?

Prototypes move in days once the hypothesis and kill criterion are written. Discovery and pilot windows are longer, because those stages produce the signal.

What happens when an experiment does not work?

It is archived against the threshold we set in advance. The outcome is written into the journal. Archive is a finished status.

How many experiments are active?

Five. Pay, Scan, and Vault are live. Signal is in pilot. Ledger is in validation.

Collaboration

Can a company partner on a pilot?

Yes, if you can put the product in a real workflow. Start on the partnerships page.

Can I invest in one product?

The lab is the entity. Products are experiments inside it. Investor notes are on the investors page.

Do you work with universities?

On questions already on the problem board, with a timeline that matches a pilot or a research note.
