Billing is still a tax on product teams

Why subscriptions, usage meters, and invoices keep getting rebuilt.

Billing looks solved until a product adds a meter. The subscription tool and the usage tool disagree about the customer, the period, and the invoice. Finance exports both into a spreadsheet. That spreadsheet is the real product.

Interviews with teams evaluating Pay kept returning to the same job: charge for a plan and for usage without operating two billing systems. Breadth of payment methods mattered less than a single ledger.

Meraxis Pay is the experiment against that finding. If design partners still reconcile outside the product after the usage release, the hypothesis is wrong and we will write that down.
