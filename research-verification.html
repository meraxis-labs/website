Verification fails at the document edge

Notes from the Meraxis Scan pilots on why identity checks break before the model runs.

The first Scan pilot measured extraction accuracy and missed the actual failure. A large share of “model errors” were photos of photos, cropped edges, and glare. The model was being asked to read a document the reviewer would also have sent back.

Moving a quality score in front of extraction changed the outcome the partner cared about: fewer false rejections, and a clear retake message instead of a generic failure. Policy mismatches — a trading name versus a legal name — remained a human decision. Scan now returns the reason, not only a pass or fail.

The open question is whether that pattern holds outside the design partner’s document mix. That is the live measure, not a benchmark on a clean dataset.
