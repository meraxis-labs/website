Meraxis Vault

Developer-first secrets and credential management. Encrypt, rotate, and audit access without the ops overhead.

The problem

Secrets end up in environment files, chat threads, and CI variables because the “correct” system is heavier than the team. Encryption is rarely the missing piece. Proof of who read or rotated a credential is.

What is live

Vault stores secrets, rotates them, and records every read, rotation, and denied access. The audit log is queryable. That was the request that survived every pilot conversation.

What we are watching

Whether teams adopt Vault for new services only, or move existing credentials. New-only adoption means the migration cost is still the product. We will treat that as a signal, not as a launch metric to explain away.
