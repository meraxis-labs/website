Case studies

Short accounts of experiments that reached users, from the problem through the decision to keep going.

Pay: one invoice for plan and usage

Design partners were reconciling two billing tools by hand. The experiment shipped usage meters onto the subscription invoice and is now measuring whether the spreadsheet disappears.

Scan: retake before reject

A three-week onboarding pilot showed that image quality, not field extraction, was the main failure. The product now scores the capture first.

Vault: audit as the feature

Pilots did not ask for a new crypto design. They asked who read a credential. The audit log shipped, and adoption is being judged on migrated secrets, not on new projects only.
